## 1. Free 3D Model Sources

### 1.1 Roblox Creator Marketplace
- **URL:** https://create.roblox.com/store/models
- **Description:** Official Roblox asset store. Free models are available under the "Free" filter. Requires a Roblox account (free) and Roblox Premium subscription to publish/sell assets, but downloading free community models is free.
- **License:** Varies per creator; most free models are royalty-free for use in Roblox games. Check individual asset pages for license terms.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Create a free Roblox account at roblox.com.
  2. Navigate to the Creator Store (create.roblox.com/store/models).
  3. Use the search bar and filter by "Free" price.
  4. Click any model to view details, then click "Download" or "Save to Roblox" to add it to your game.
- **Notes:** Models are optimized for Roblox Studio. Part counts should be kept under 500 for performance. Use SurfaceAppearance for PBR materials.

### 1.2 Meshy.ai (Roblox Category)
- **URL:** https://www.meshy.ai/subcategory/roblox
- **Description:** AI-powered 3D model generator with a dedicated Roblox category. Offers 232+ free Roblox models (characters, props, environments) in STL, FBX, GLB, OBJ, USDZ formats.
- **License:** CC0 (Creative Commons Zero) — royalty-free for commercial use, no attribution required.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Visit meshy.ai/subcategory/roblox.
  2. Browse the gallery of 232+ free models.
  3. Click a model to open its detail page.
  4. Download in your preferred format (FBX/GLB recommended for Roblox Studio).
  5. No signup required for downloads.
- **Notes:** Also offers AI generation — describe a Roblox model in text and get a textured, download-ready 3D model in seconds. Free plan generates CC BY 4.0 licensed models (requires crediting Meshy); pre-made gallery models are CC0.

### 1.3 Sketchfab (Free / CC0)
- **URL:** https://sketchfab.com/tags/cc0 and https://sketchfab.com/tags/roblox
- **Description:** Largest online 3D model repository. The CC0 tag filters for public-domain models. The Roblox tag has community-uploaded Roblox-compatible models. Requires a free account to download.
- **License:** Varies by creator; CC0-tagged models are public domain. Non-CC0 models may require attribution or have restricted commercial use — always check the individual model's license.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Create a free account at sketchfab.com.
  2. Search for "roblox" or browse the CC0 tag.
  3. Click a model, then click "Download" on the detail page.
  4. Choose format (FBX, OBJ, GLB, STL).
  5. Free models are downloadable; paid models require purchase.
- **Notes:** Sketchfab also has a "Free 3D Models" tag and a "Downloadable" filter. The CC0 Public Domain collection by user "plaggy" contains 193 models.

### 1.4 Poly Haven
- **URL:** https://polyhaven.com/models
- **Description:** Curated public 3D asset library with 521+ free models, 855+ textures, and 1,063+ HDRIs. All assets are CC0 — no login required, no attribution needed.
- **License:** CC0 (Public Domain) — use for any purpose, commercial or non-commercial, without restrictions.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Visit polyhaven.com/models.
  2. Browse or search for models by name/category.
  3. Click a model to open its detail page.
  4. Download links are available directly on the page (no account needed).
  5. Models come in GLB/GLTF format, ready for import into Roblox Studio or other engines.
- **Notes:** Poly Haven also offers a Blender add-on for direct asset importing, and an Unreal Engine HDRI browser plugin. All assets are made by human artists, avoiding generative AI and procedural methods.

### 1.5 Free3D.com
- **URL:** https://free3d.com/3d-models/poly
- **Description:** Large repository of free 3D models. Search for "poly" or "roblox" categories. Requires a free account for downloads.
- **License:** Varies per model — check individual pages. Many models are free for personal and commercial use with attribution.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Visit free3d.com.
  2. Search for models by keyword or browse categories.
  3. Create a free account to download.
  4. Choose from available formats (BLEND, OBJ, C4D, 3DS, MAX, MA).
- **Notes:** 756+ free Poly models available. Quality varies — filter by rating and downloads.

### 1.6 BuiltByBit
- **URL:** https://builtbybit.com/resources/roblox/models/
- **Description:** Community-driven Roblox model resource site with extensive collections of free and premium Roblox models.
- **License:** Varies — check individual resources.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Visit builtbybit.com/resources/roblox/models/.
  2. Browse categories or search for specific model types.
  3. Click a resource to view details and download links.
- **Notes:** Some resources may require a free account. Quality is generally high with community ratings.

### 1.7 RobloxBay
- **URL:** https://robloxbay.com/roblox-models
- **Description:** Free, community-driven marketplace for Roblox developer assets. Offers 3D models, scripts, maps, UI kits, and game templates. All assets are reviewed by moderation before going live.
- **License:** Community-driven; check individual asset pages for license terms. DMCA takedown policy enforced.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Visit robloxbay.com/roblox-models.
  2. Browse by category or search by keyword.
  3. Click an asset to view details, ratings, and changelogs.
  4. Download for free (Pro and Ultimate tiers offer higher daily limits).

### 1.8 CGTrader (Free Models)
- **URL:** https://www.cgtrader.com/3d-models/roblox
- **Description:** Large 3D model marketplace with a Roblox category. Free models available with account creation.

## 2. Free Texture Pack Sources

### 2.1 Poly Haven (Textures)
- **URL:** https://polyhaven.com/textures
- **Description:** 855+ free PBR texture sets in categories: aerial, terrain, floor, brick, wood, plaster/concrete, fabric, metal, roofing, rock. All CC0, no login required.
- **License:** CC0 (Public Domain) — no attribution required, commercial use allowed.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Visit polyhaven.com/textures.
  2. Browse by category or search by keyword.
  3. Click a texture set to open its detail page.
  4. Download individual maps (Albedo, Normal, Roughness, AO, Displacement) or the full pack.
  5. Available resolutions: 2K, 4K, 8K.
- **Notes:** Includes terrain textures (grass, dirt, stone, sand, snow), wood, stone, metal, and more. Also provides HDRIs for environment lighting.

### 2.2 ambientCG
- **URL:** https://ambientcg.com/
- **Description:** 2,000+ free PBR materials, HDRIs, and 3D models. All CC0 Public Domain. Over 1 million monthly downloads. Categories include Tiles, Paving Stones, Wood, Metal, Stone, Concrete, Fabric, Leather, Snow, Lava, and more.
- **License:** CC0 — free to use without attribution, even in commercial circumstances.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Visit ambientcg.com.
  2. Browse by category or search by keyword.
  3. Click a material to open its detail page.
  4. Download individual PBR maps (Albedo, Normal, Roughness, AO, Displacement) or the full substance package.
  5. Resolutions up to 8K available.
- **Notes:** Includes Substance Designer files (.sbsar) for procedural customization. Covers terrain, resource, and environment textures needed for the simulator.

### 2.3 Textures.com (Free Tier)
- **URL:** https://www.textures.com/free
- **Description:** Veteran texture library with thousands of PBR materials. Free tier provides daily credits for downloading high-res textures. Categories include brick, wood, stone, metal, fabric, terrain, sci-fi, and more.

- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Create a free account at textures.com.
  2. Navigate to the Free section or browse categories.
  3. Use daily credits to download textures.
  4. Choose resolution (up to 16K for some free textures).
  5. Download in JPG, PNG, or EXR formats.
- **Notes:** Free account receives daily credits. Premium access ($) unlocks full library. Textures include full PBR map sets (Albedo, Normal, Roughness, AO, Displacement).

## 3. Free AI Image Generators for Design Concepts

### 3.1 Google Gemini (Nano Banana 2 / Gemini 3.1 Flash Image)
- **URL:** https://gemini.google.com/
- **Description:** Google's latest image generation model. Free users get approximately 20 images per day at up to 1K resolution. Excellent for concept art, UI mockups, and design exploration.
- **License:** Google's terms apply. Outputs carry SynthID invisible watermark and C2PA content credentials. Check current Google terms for commercial use permissions.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Access Gemini at gemini.google.com.
  2. Sign in with a Google account.
  3. Use the image generation feature (type "/imagine" or use the image creation tool).
  4. Enter your prompt and generate.
  5. Download the generated image.
- **Notes:** Best free daily allowance among cloud-based tools. Handles complex text rendering in over 10 languages.

### 3.2 ChatGPT Images 2.0 (GPT Image 2)
- **URL:** https://chat.openai.com/
- **Description:** OpenAI's latest image generation model integrated into ChatGPT. Free tier gives approximately 2-3 images per 24-hour rolling window. Excellent for conversational iteration — refine images using natural language.
- **License:** OpenAI's terms apply. Check current OpenAI terms for commercial use permissions on free tier.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Access ChatGPT at chat.openai.com.
  2. Sign in with a free OpenAI account.
  3. Use the image generation feature in the chat interface.
  4. Describe what you want and iterate naturally.
  5. Download generated images from the chat.
- **Notes:** DALL-E 3 was deprecated on May 12, 2026. ChatGPT Images 2.0 is the current model. Best for beginners and conversational editing.

### 3.3 Microsoft Designer / Bing Image Creator
- **URL:** https://www.microsoft.com/designer
- **Description:** Powered by DALL-E 3 under the hood. 15 fast creations per day, unlimited slow queue. Also offers MAI-Image-2e (Microsoft's own model) and GPT-4o as model options.
- **License:** Free outputs restricted to non-commercial personal use under Microsoft's current terms. Requires a personal Microsoft account.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Visit microsoft.com/designer or bing.com/create.
  2. Sign in with a Microsoft account.
  3. Enter a prompt and generate.
  4. Download the generated image (1024x1024, square format).
- **Notes:** Outputs carry a visible watermark. DALL-E 3 is being phased out in favor of MAI-Image-2e.

### 3.4 Leonardo AI
- **URL:** https://leonardo.ai/
- **Description:** Professional-grade image generation with 150 tokens/day (roughly 30-50 images). Specialized models for game assets, character designs, environment art, item icons, and texture maps. Includes custom model training on free tier.
- **License:** Free users receive a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use images commercially. Leonardo retains rights to use, reproduce, and distribute free-plan images (public by default). Paid plans include full ownership and private generation.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Create a free account at leonardo.ai.
  2. Choose a model (Phoenix, Vision XL, or community fine-tuned models).
  3. Enter a prompt and generate.
  4. Download the generated image.
- **Notes:** Best free option for game asset concept art. The token system allows more daily generations than most competitors.

### 3.5 Stable Diffusion (Local / Open Source)
- **URL:** https://stablediffusionweb.com/ (online) or https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI (local)
- **Description:** Open-source image generation model. No platform cap after setup. Run locally with ComfyUI or AUTOMATIC1111 for unlimited free generation. SD 3.5 is the current version.
- **License:** Stability AI Community License — free commercial use for individuals and organizations with annual revenues under $1 million. Larger organizations need an enterprise license.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. For local use: Install ComfyUI or AUTOMATIC1111 following the setup guide.
  2. Download model weights (SD 3.5 Medium needs ~10GB VRAM, Large needs ~18GB).
  3. Install a frontend and run locally.
  4. For browser-based: Use stablediffusionweb.com (no account needed).
- **Notes:** Truly unlimited, no watermarks, complete privacy. Requires GPU with 8GB+ VRAM. Best for power users who want full control.

### 3.6 FLUX (via Mage.Space or Hugging Face)
- **URL:** https://mage.space/ or https://huggingface.co/spaces (search for FLUX)
- **Description:** Black Forest Labs' open-weight image generation model. FLUX.1 Schnell and FLUX.2 Klein (4B) are Apache 2.0 licensed — free commercial use. Generates images in 1-3 seconds.
- **License:** Apache 2.0 for Schnell and Klein variants. Dev variants carry non-commercial licenses.
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Visit mage.space or a Hugging Face Space hosting FLUX.
  2. Enter a prompt and generate.
  3. Download the generated image.
  4. No account required for most hosting platforms.
- **Notes:** Best open-weight option for browser-based generation. Quality rivals models taking 10x longer to generate.

### 3.7 Ideogram
- **URL:** https://ideogram.ai/
- **Description:** Best-in-class text rendering inside generated images. Free tier provides 10 slow credits per week (resetting every Saturday). Each credit covers up to 4 image variations (~40 images/week).
- **License:** Free-plan images are public by default. Commercial use permitted on free tier (except competitor model training).
- **Download Instructions:**
  1. Create a free account at ideogram.ai.
  2. Enter a prompt and generate.
  3. Download the generated image.
- **Notes:** Ideal for generating UI mockups with readable text, logos, and signage for the simulator's design concepts.

## 7. Asset Creation Workflow

### Recommended Pipeline
1. **Concept Phase:** Use AI image generators (Stable Diffusion, Leonardo AI, ChatGPT Images) to create concept art for characters, pets, tools, and environments.
2. **Modeling Phase:** Download base models from Meshy.ai, Sketchfab, and Poly Haven. Import into Blender or Roblox Studio for customization.
3. **Texture Phase:** Download PBR texture sets from Poly Haven, ambientCG, and 3DTextures.me. Apply to models in Blender or Roblox Studio's SurfaceAppearance system.
4. **Optimization Phase:** Optimize models for Roblox performance (under 500 parts, clean UVs, proper scaling where 1 stud = 0.28 meters).
5. **Integration Phase:** Import finalized models and textures into Roblox Studio. Test in-game appearance and performance.
